
# Development history

A month-by-month overview of how Momo Movies has grown. Entries summarize
the significant work of each month rather than tracking individual releases.

## October 2026 — Refining Discover

Discover's controls became more deliberate: a maintained streaming-service
catalog with clearer service selection, a scope control for signed-in users
(Everything, My watchlist, or New to me), and the Frame bands reorganized
into an expandable Selectivity bar so combinations can be mixed freely.
Behind the scenes, viewing-intensity calibration was tightened with
adjudicated scores, and a review-to-publication path was built for approved
catalog artwork.

## September 2026 — A fuller Discover language, faster

Discover grew from broad browsing into composable refinement: release era,
genre, your relationship to a title, Involvement-based ordering, reputation,
runtime, and preference controls, with browsing position preserved while
controls change. The month also focused on speed and legibility: fewer
first-load requests, faster poster loading, better result batching, and a
Highlights layout that makes the recommendation model more visible.

## August 2026 — Richer titles, safer foundations

Title pages gained clearer watch options, provider grouping,
audience-reception context, recognition evidence, and people and credits,
alongside an editorial artwork gallery. Underneath, a reviewed provider
taxonomy and provider-selection overlay were established, and the public
browsing layer was deliberately separated and hardened against the private
source material behind it.

## July 2026 — First public release

Momo Movies launched. The project began on July 8 with a curated
recommendation seed and a canonical-data direction; the first public shell
was deployed July 9 with a browse-first interface and no public login or
full database — a deliberate choice to validate a clear, useful experience
first. The frontend was then rebuilt as a Svelte prototype with reusable,
tested browse components. The rest of the month built the product out:
an early three-part Vibes model (Good time, Escape, Reflection) for
browsing by mood, a personal library with owned-copy controls and a
near-term queue, catalog expansion with a CSV import path, Google sign-in,
and the first forms of the availability presentation the app still uses.

## Before the app — 2017–2021

Momo Movies began as a personal movie database that evolved into a
spreadsheet-based recommendation system, built from years of hand-tracked
viewing. That history — and the habit of separating private data
preparation from a deliberately curated public experience — shapes the
app to this day.
